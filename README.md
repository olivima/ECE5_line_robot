<h3>Version 1 - Initial Prototype | August 25</h3>
<p>
    Our initial prototype includes our first 3D-printed chassis design to get our components
    aligned & assembled into one place. This version could follow a line and gave us a good 
    starting point for our subsequent improvements.
<p>

<h3>Version 2 - Component Reorganization | August 26</h3>
<p>
  We revised the chassis so that we could rearrange component placement for the robot to improve 
  it's organization, including fitting the battery inside the chassis' lower shelf.
<p>

<h3>Version 3 - Traction & Light Shield | August 27</h3>
<p>
  We added a light shield around the photoresistor breadboard to reduce ambient light 
  interference, and wired LEDs on the breadboard & aluminum foiled the shield's 
  interior to provide a constant light source for more consistent calibration & readings. 
  We also added rubber bands to the wheels to improve traction on the poster boards. 
<p>

<h3>Version 4 - Major Mechanical & Electrical Redesign | September 1</h3>
<p>
  Version 4 was our largest redesign. We replaced the photoresistor/LED and potentiometer 
  breadboards with soldered protoboards, created a custom 3D-printed light shield for
  the photoresistor/LED board, and majorly updated our chassis to support this cleaner
  organization.
<p>

<h3>Version 5 - Final Design | September 2</h3>
<p>
  Our final version! It incorporates some mechanical improvements, including a slightly 
  shorter chassis and integrating the light shield directly into the same body design file, 
  which eliminated the need for hot glue and improving alignment. We also added thin motor 
  braces to the roof of the first shelf to keep the motors flush against the chassis
  wall and prevent the motors and wheels from tilting during movement.
</p>
